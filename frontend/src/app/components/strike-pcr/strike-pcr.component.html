PCR Analysis Reference & Guidelines

Formulas
  • PCR (OI): Put OI ÷ Call OI (Values > 1.0 indicate bullish put writing bias, < 1.0 indicate bearish call writing bias).
  • PCR (Change in OI): Put Change in OI ÷ Call Change in OI (Measures intraday positional accumulation & momentum shifts).
  • Safe Division: Missing strikes and zero denominators are safely handled without producing NaN or runtime errors.
Sentiment Interpretation
  • PCR > 1.20 Bullish: Traders selling aggressive puts as support.
  • 0.80 - 1.20 Neutral / Range-bound: Balanced call and put positioning.
  • PCR < 0.80 Bearish: Traders selling heavy calls creating overhead resistance.
Market Direction Rules
  • Call Inc + Put Dec BEARISH PRESSURE: Stronger resistance + relatively weaker support (Red).
  • Call Dec + Put Inc BULLISH PRESSURE: Weaker resistance + stronger support (Green).
  • Call Inc + Put Inc MIXED / RANGE-BOUND: Both resistance and support may be increasing (Yellow/Orange).
  • Call Dec + Put Dec NEUTRAL / UNCLEAR: Both resistance and support weakening; no clear directional signal (Gray).
