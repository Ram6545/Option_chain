Options Put-Call Ratio (PCR) Educational Interpretation & Context

Standard Formula

PCR = Total Put Open Interest / Total Call Open Interest

verified_user Safe Division Engine: Zero or missing Call OI is handled gracefully (returns N/A), preventing misleading mathematical infinities or corrupted metrics.

Rising PCR Dynamic

When PCR increases, put open interest is accumulating faster than call open interest. In derivatives markets, this often indicates aggressive put writing by institutional participants, establishing a supportive floor under the index.

Falling PCR Dynamic

When PCR decreases, call open interest is building relative to put open interest. This indicates call writing and hedging against downward movement, signaling potential overhead resistance and selling pressure.

Configurable Thresholds

Current configured thresholds: Bullish ≥ {{ bullishThreshold() }} | Bearish ≤ {{ bearishThreshold() }}. Market regimes differ between weekly and monthly expiries; thresholds can be adjusted dynamically in the top control bar.

info
Trading Notice & Risk Context: Put-Call Ratio alone should never be treated as an isolated or standalone buy/sell signal. PCR must be evaluated alongside underlying price structure, multi-strike open interest shifts, market volatility (India VIX), and overall macroeconomic backdrop. Inferred support and resistance zones are probabilistic concentration levels, not guaranteed outcomes.
